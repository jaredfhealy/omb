Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Operation More Bacon ("OMB", "we", "our", or "us") is a local-first personal finance and options-income projection application. This Privacy Policy explains what data OMB processes and how it is handled.

1. Information We Process

OMB processes data that you choose to enter or import, including:

  • Account settings and balances
  • Recurring transactions
  • Imported brokerage transaction rows and position information
  • Notes and reporting-related data generated from your inputs

2. Local-First Data Storage

By default, application data is stored locally in your browser/device (for example, IndexedDB and local browser storage). OMB does not require a central OMB-hosted cloud database to operate.

3. Optional Google Drive Sync

If you choose to connect Google Drive, OMB may store encrypted or plain JSON app state files in your Google Drive appDataFolder using your authorization.

OMB requests only the Google OAuth scope needed for app-specific data storage (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata).

You may disconnect Drive sync at any time. Disconnect operations can remove OMB appData files based on in-app actions.

4. No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Server-Side Processing

OMB is designed as a local-first application. We do not operate a dedicated OMB cloud service that stores your finance data. Third-party services you choose (such as Google Drive) process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to reduce risk, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. You are responsible for securing your device, browser profile, and Google account.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local data remains on your device until you remove it (for example by browser data clearing or in-app overwrite/deletion flows). Drive data remains in your Google account until deleted through OMB controls or Google Drive tools.

8. Children's Privacy

OMB is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
